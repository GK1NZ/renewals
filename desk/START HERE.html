Broker Desk

One app for the whole job: prospects, client information, the market, quotes, the recommendation, placement and income. It runs from this folder. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Open Broker Desk

Starting

  1. Keep this folder together, anywhere you like: Documents, a USB stick, or a shared drive.
  2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Bookmark it.
  3. Two short screens ask for your name and the insurers you use. Only your name is needed.
  4. A one-minute tour shows you around, and a Getting started list on the Today screen walks you through the first jobs and ticks them off as you go. The Help button explains whichever screen you are on.

Where your work is kept

Everything stays in the browser on the device you are using. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Back up weekly. Use Back up now at the bottom of the page. It saves one file holding every client, quote, document and setting. Broker Desk reminds you when a week has passed. Clearing the browser's site data, or using a private window, removes the work, so the backup file is your safety net.

Automatic backup to OneDrive or Google Drive

On a computer, in Chrome or Edge: open Settings, choose Choose backup folder, and pick a folder inside your OneDrive or Google Drive folder. From then on every change is saved there within a few seconds and your cloud drive copies it up. The browser may ask you to allow the folder again after a restart; one tap on Resume at the bottom of the page does it.

On a phone, Back up now opens the share list so you can send the file straight to Google Drive or OneDrive.

If a device is lost: open Broker Desk on the new one, choose Restore from a backup file, and pick BrokerDesk-backup-latest.json from your cloud drive.

Moving to another computer, phone or firm

  1. Back up on the old device.
  2. Copy this folder to the new one and open index.html.
  3. On the first screen choose Restore from a backup file.

A phone and a laptop each hold their own copy. To carry work between them, back up on one and restore on the other.

What is in the folder

index.htmlBroker Desk itself.
meeting-guide.htmlThe Meeting Guide. Opens from Start meeting and saves back to the client.
client-formThe form clients fill in themselves. To use it, put this whole folder on any web host, then paste the form's web address into Settings.
data, libAddress look-up data, industry questions and the PDF and spreadsheet tools. Leave these in place.

Email and reminders

Broker Desk never sends anything. On a computer each email is saved as a draft file that opens in Outlook or Apple Mail, ready to check and send, with formatting and attachments. On a phone it opens your mail app. Reminders save as calendar files. Change this in Settings.

Site look-up

In the Meeting Guide, pick the address from the list to get the land parcel, building footprint, council flood layers and a satellite link. These go into the underwriter submission. Address data covers the South Island and Auckland. Flood layers cover Christchurch, Selwyn and Waimakariri. With a LINZ Basemaps key in Settings, an aerial photo is added too.

Address, parcel and building data: LINZ, CC BY 4.0. Flood layers: Christchurch City Council and Waimakariri District Council open data. All indicative only.

Sharing with a team

For a shared register kept in OneDrive, with drafts created straight in Outlook, choose Use Microsoft 365 instead on the first screen. Your IT provider needs to register the app once in Microsoft Entra and give you the Application ID.
